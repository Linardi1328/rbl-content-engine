Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RBL Productions handles information when RBL Content Engine is used to authorize and publish to supported social platforms.

1. Information this public website collects

This informational website does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics tracking, or data-entry forms. Standard hosting infrastructure may process basic request information such as IP address, browser information, requested path, and security logs as necessary to deliver and protect the site.

2. Information the desktop application may access

When you explicitly authorize a supported social account, the application may receive information permitted by the scopes you approve. For TikTok, the intended permissions are limited to basic account identity information and video publishing authorization, including user.info.basic and video.publish.

The application may also process publication metadata that you provide or approve, such as video files, captions, privacy settings, platform controls, provider publication identifiers, and publication status.

3. OAuth credentials

RBL Content Engine does not ask for or store your social account password. Authorization is performed through the social platform's OAuth flow. Where a refresh token is required for continued authorized access, the desktop workflow stores it in local application state on the operator's device. Short-lived access tokens may be used transiently to make authorized API requests.

4. How information is used

Authorized information is used only to identify the connected account, query settings required for a publication workflow, upload or publish content that has been approved for that account, and reconcile the result of a publication request.

5. Sharing

Information is sent to the relevant connected social platform when necessary to perform an authorized API operation. RBL Productions does not sell personal information or share social account authorization data with advertising data brokers.

6. Retention

Local authorization state is retained only while it is needed to maintain the authorized integration. Publication receipts may retain provider identifiers, timestamps, and status information so the application can avoid accidental duplicate submissions and maintain an operational record. Public website hosting logs may be retained according to the hosting provider's security and operational policies.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke a connected application's authorization from the relevant social platform's account settings. After revocation, local authorization state associated with that platform should be deleted from the application environment. Requests concerning RBL-controlled publication records or other locally held data can be submitted through the support channel below.

8. Security

Credentials and authorization tokens are not intentionally committed to the public source repository or exposed through this website. The application is designed to keep runtime authentication state separate from tracked project files.

9. Children's privacy

RBL Content Engine is not designed as a service for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

10. Third-party services

Connected social platforms process information under their own privacy policies. Their practices are separate from this Privacy Policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests can be submitted through the project's public support channel at GitHub Issues.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data flows, platform permissions, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
