Creator publishing workflow

Approved content, published through authorized accounts.

RBL Content Engine is a desktop-first workflow for preparing, reviewing, scheduling, and publishing video content to social platforms using official APIs and explicit account authorization.

Review before publish

Video, caption, privacy settings, and platform-specific controls are prepared before a publication action is submitted.

Official authorization

Connected social accounts authorize access through platform OAuth flows. Credentials are not embedded in public website code.

Platform-aware publishing

The workflow respects supported platform settings, publishing permissions, status checks, and content requirements.

How it works

From final video to publication receipt.

1

Prepare and review

The creator reviews the final media, caption, privacy choice, and relevant platform settings.

2

Authorize the social account

The account owner grants the required permissions using the social platform's official OAuth authorization flow.

3

Publish through the platform API

Approved content is submitted through the platform's official publishing API and its provider status is tracked.

4

Record the result

The application records publication state and provider identifiers so uncertain writes are not blindly duplicated.

Privacy & security

Minimal access, explicit authorization.

OAuth-based access

Social account access is granted by the account owner. The public website does not collect social account passwords.

Local application state

Where supported, long-lived authorization state needed by the desktop workflow is stored locally rather than exposed through this public site.

No ad tracking

This public information site does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or account signup forms.
